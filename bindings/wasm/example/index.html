Bezier — WebAssembly demo

Controls

Click a curveselect it (Shift+click adds; empty space clears)
Drag a handlemove a control point of a selected curve
Scrollzoom in / out (centered on the cursor)
Drag empty spacepan the view
0reset zoom / pan
Nnew curve: click to place control points, Enter to finish, Esc to cancel
Ffreehand: drag to draw a stroke, release to fit a curve
Del / Backspacedelete selected curves
↑ / ↓raise / lower order of selected
O + ↑/↓increase / decrease offset-curve distance (hold O)
Btoggle bounding boxes
Etoggle extrema
Itoggle intersections (between curves and self-intersections)
Ptoggle projection: tangent / normal at cursor
Jfit-join exactly two selected curves into one
H / ?toggle this help
Rreset the scene
